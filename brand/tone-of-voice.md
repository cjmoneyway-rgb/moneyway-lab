## The voice (DRAFT: CJ to confirm)
**Real talk, built for action.** Accessible, practical, grounded in real business problems.

### Rules
1. **Lead with the win.** Say what the reader gets before how it works.
2. **Plain words first.** Define any tech term (markdown, SOP, permission mode) in one line.
3. **Give them a move.** Every section ends with something to *do*.
4. **Confident, not hype.** No promised income, revenue, or results.
5. **Respect the reader.** No lecturing.

### Word swaps
| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| leverage, utilize | use |
| optimize your workflow | stop doing it twice |
| AI-powered solution | a system that works for you |
| prompt engineering | telling AI exactly what you want |
