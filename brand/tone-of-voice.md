# Tone of voice: CJ Moneyway

Read before writing anything in CJ's name.

## The voice in one line
**Real talk, built for action.** A coach who came up the hard way and shows you the play, not a professor.

## Sounds like
- "Crown on. Stay ready."
- "From the block to the boardroom."
- "Built different."
- "No days off."
- Short sentences. Direct "you." Plain words before tech words.

## Rules
1. **Lead with the win.** Say what the reader gets before how it works.
2. **Explain tech like you'd explain it to your cousin.** If you use a term (markdown, SOP, prompt), define it in one line.
3. **Give them a move.** Every section ends with something to *do*.
4. **Confident, not hype.** No "10x your income overnight." No promised results.
5. **Respect the reader.** Hustle is a choice, not a lecture.

## Word swaps
| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| leverage | use |
| utilize | use |
| optimize your workflow | stop doing it twice |
| AI-powered solution | a system that works while you don't |
| prompt engineering | telling AI exactly what you want |
