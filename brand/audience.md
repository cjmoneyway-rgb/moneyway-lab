# Audience

Read only when writing sales pages, workshop invites, or marketing copy.

## Primary (draft, CJ to confirm)
- Creators, hustlers, and small business owners who already have *something*
  (a skill, a story, a side business) and want AI to help them turn it into income.
- Not engineers. They're comfortable on a phone and may never have opened a terminal.
- Listeners of The CJ Moneyway Show and readers of *Both Eyes Open and Both Eyes Shut*.

## What they want
- To stop starting over every time they use AI
- Systems that save hours each week
- Products they can sell: workbooks, workshops, content

## What stops them
- "AI is for tech people."
- Generic AI output that doesn't sound like them
- Too many tools, no plan
