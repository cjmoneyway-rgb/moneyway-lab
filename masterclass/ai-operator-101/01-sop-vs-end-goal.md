# #1 Know your instruction type: SOP vs end goal

> **The win:** Stop getting the wrong thing back. Half of bad AI output comes from mixing up two kinds of instructions.

## Two kinds of instructions

```
                 Two kinds of instructions
                 ┌───────────┴───────────┐
               SOP                    END GOAL
  Standard Operating Procedure    Where you want to end up
     "Follow these steps."         "Get me there. Figure out how."
```

**SOP (Standard Operating Procedure):** you already know the steps. AI follows them the same way every time.
Use it for repeat work: processing leads, formatting episodes, posting clips.

**End goal:** you know the *destination* and let AI pick the route.
Use it for creative or new work: a workshop outline, a sales page, a new product idea.

## SOP example: the lead machine

```
[Contact form] ──▶ [CRM] ──▶ < Qualified? > ──Yes──▶ Email A: "Let's book a call"
                                   │
                                   └──No───▶ Email B: "Here's a free guide"
```

Someone fills out your form, they land in your CRM (customer list) as **New**, and one question decides
which email they get. Same steps every time. That's an SOP.

### The Moneyway version
```
[Workshop sign-up] ──▶ [Email list] ──▶ < Bought the book? >
                                           ├─Yes─▶ "Here's your workbook upgrade"
                                           └─No──▶ "Free Chapter 1 + podcast episode"
```

## End goal example
> "I want a 90-minute workshop based on my book that people leave with a written plan. Draft the outline."

You didn't give steps. You gave a finish line.

## The mistake
- Giving **steps** when you wanted creativity, so you get a robot result.
- Giving a **vague goal** when you needed it done exactly the same way, so you get a different result every time.
