## The goal prompt: 4 parts
```
GOAL      What are we making and why?
CONTEXT   What should you read or know first?
LIMITS    What must it be / must it never be?
DONE =    How will we both know it's finished?
```

## Weak vs strong
**Weak:**
> "Make me a workbook from my book."

**Strong:**
> **GOAL:** Draft a 10-page companion workbook for *Both Eyes Open and Both Eyes Shut* that readers can finish in a weekend and that leads into my live workshop.
> **CONTEXT:** Read `brand/tone-of-voice.md`, `brand/audience.md`, and the chapter notes in `products/book/`. Prices are in `brand/facts.md`.
> **LIMITS:** One exercise per chapter. Plain language. Don't invent quotes from the book; mark gaps as TODO. No promised income results.
> **DONE =** A markdown file in `products/workbooks/` with a cover page, one exercise per chapter, and a final page inviting readers to the workshop. Plan first and ask me questions before writing.
