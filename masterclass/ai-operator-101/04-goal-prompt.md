# Move #4: Brief it with a goal prompt

**AI Operator 101 · Moneyway Lab™ · CJ Moneyway Media™**

> **The win:** Brief AI the way you'd brief a new hire on day one, and get back work you'd sign your name to.

> **Original framework.** The four-part goal prompt (GOAL · CONTEXT · LIMITS · DONE LOOKS LIKE) is
> Moneyway Lab™'s own teaching framework. The outside tutorial this course drew on showed only the
> heading "Brief it with a goal prompt". Nothing below comes from it.

## Learning objective
By the end of this lesson you can:
1. Name and explain the four parts of the Moneyway goal prompt.
2. Turn a weak one-line request into a complete four-part brief.
3. Write a "done looks like" that you can actually check.

## Explanation

### The four parts
| Part | Question it answers | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. GOAL** | What are we making, for whom, and why? | "Write a post." | "Write an Instagram caption announcing our fall candle line to returning customers so they pre-order." |
| **2. CONTEXT** | What should AI read or know first? | *(nothing)* | "Read tone-of-voice.md, offer.md, and facts.md. Last fall's caption is in posts/2025-fall.md." |
| **3. LIMITS** | What must it be, and what must it never be? | *(nothing)* | "Under 120 words. One emoji max. Prices only from facts.md. No discount codes. No 'best candle ever' claims." |
| **4. DONE LOOKS LIKE** | How will we both know it's finished? | *(nothing)* | "Three caption options in drafts/fall-caption.md, each with a call to action and alt text for the photo." |

**Tips**
- **GOAL** names the *audience* and the *purpose*, not just the format.
- **CONTEXT** points to files (Move #2) instead of retyping them.
- **LIMITS** includes the risk rules from Move #3: no invented facts, no sending, no prices from memory.
- **DONE LOOKS LIKE** is checkable: file name, location, count, and the parts it must have.
- For anything bigger than a quick draft, add: **"Plan first and ask me questions before you build."**

### How the four moves stack
```
#1 Pick the type  →  #2 Memory loaded  →  #3 Plan first  →  #4 Goal prompt
   (SOP or goal)       (CLAUDE.md files)     (Plan mode)         (the brief)
```

## Weak vs strong: three businesses
**Entrepreneur (coaching business)**
- Weak: "Make me a lead magnet."
- Strong:
  - **GOAL:** a 1-page checklist, "5 Signs Your Side Hustle Is Ready to Register," for first-time founders, to grow my email list.
  - **CONTEXT:** audience.md and tone-of-voice.md.
  - **LIMITS:** no legal or tax advice. Add a line telling readers to check with a local professional. Plain language.
  - **DONE LOOKS LIKE:** `lead-magnet.md` with a title, 5 items of 2 sentences each, and a 1-line call to action. Ready for my review.

**Creator (podcast or YouTube)**
- Weak: "Write show notes."
- Strong:
  - **GOAL:** show notes for episode 14 that help new listeners decide to press play.
  - **CONTEXT:** the transcript in `episodes/ep14.txt`, plus tone-of-voice.md.
  - **LIMITS:** quote the guest only word-for-word from the transcript. No made-up timestamps. Under 250 words.
  - **DONE LOOKS LIKE:** a 2-sentence hook, 3–5 timestamped topics taken from the transcript, guest links from facts.md, saved to `episodes/ep14-notes.md`.

**Small-business owner (local service)**
- Weak: "Reply to this bad review."
- Strong:
  - **GOAL:** a calm, professional public reply that shows future customers we care.
  - **CONTEXT:** the review text (customer name removed) and tone-of-voice.md.
  - **LIMITS:** don't admit legal fault, don't share any customer details, don't offer refunds (I decide that). Under 80 words.
  - **DONE LOOKS LIKE:** two reply drafts for me to choose from. I post it myself.

## Worked example (completed)
*Fictional business: **Brick & Bloom Candle Co.***

**Before:** "Make a wholesale price sheet."

**After:**
```
GOAL:            A one-page wholesale price sheet for boutique owners, so they can
                 place a first order without a phone call.
CONTEXT:         Read brand.md, tone-of-voice.md, offer.md, and facts.md
                 (wholesale prices and minimum order live there).
LIMITS:          Prices and minimums only from facts.md. If one is missing,
                 write TODO and ask me. Don't invent product names. Black + one
                 brand color. Printable on letter paper.
DONE LOOKS LIKE: wholesale/price-sheet.md with a header, a product table
                 (name · scent · case size · price), the minimum order, how to
                 order, and contact info from facts.md. Draft for my review.

Plan first and ask me questions before you build.
```

## Exercise
1. Copy one weak prompt you've actually used (or use: "Write a newsletter").
2. Rewrite it with all four parts. Use the fictional shop if the real one involves private details.
3. Read your DONE LOOKS LIKE aloud. Could someone else check it with a yes/no? If not, make it more specific.
4. Add "Plan first and ask me questions before you build" if it's more than a quick draft.

Use Workbook Exercise 4 or `templates/goal-prompt.md`.

## Expected output
One four-part goal prompt in which:
- GOAL names an audience and a purpose,
- CONTEXT points to at least one file,
- LIMITS has at least one "never" rule,
- DONE LOOKS LIKE names a file or format and its required parts.
